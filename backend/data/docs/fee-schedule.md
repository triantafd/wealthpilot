# Schedule of Fees and Charges

**WealthPilot Advisers Ltd** — fictional firm, synthetic document. The figures
below are invented and bear no relation to any real firm's pricing.

## 1. Ongoing advisory fee

The advisory fee is charged on the total value of all accounts a client holds
with the firm, and is **tiered**: each band applies only to the portion of value
that falls within it.

| Portfolio value | Annual rate |
|---|---|
| First £500,000 | 1.00% |
| £500,001 – £1,000,000 | 0.85% |
| £1,000,001 – £2,000,000 | 0.70% |
| £2,000,001 – £5,000,000 | 0.55% |
| Above £5,000,000 | 0.40% |

The minimum annual advisory fee is **£1,500**, regardless of portfolio value.
There is no maximum.

### Worked example

A client with £1,400,000 across two accounts pays:

- £500,000 at 1.00% = £5,000
- £500,000 at 0.85% = £4,250
- £400,000 at 0.70% = £2,800

Total advisory fee: **£12,050 per year**, an effective rate of 0.861%.

Note that the fee is tiered, not banded: a client with £1,400,000 does **not**
pay 0.70% on the whole amount.

## 2. Platform fee

The platform fee covers custody, settlement, reporting and online access.

| Portfolio value | Annual rate |
|---|---|
| First £1,000,000 | 0.25% |
| Above £1,000,000 | 0.10% |

The platform fee is **capped at £1,200 per year** per client, however many
accounts they hold.

## 3. Transaction charges

| Transaction | Charge |
|---|---|
| Equity or ETF purchase or sale | £9.95 per trade |
| Fund purchase or sale | No charge |
| Telephone-dealt trade | £29.95 per trade |
| Stock transfer in or out | £15.00 per line |
| Full account transfer out | £75.00 |

Rebalancing trades generated by the firm's quarterly review are **exempt from
transaction charges**. Trades requested by the client are not.

## 4. Fund ongoing charges

Each fund levies its own ongoing charges figure (OCF), deducted inside the fund
and reflected in its unit price. The firm does not receive any part of it.

| Fund type | Typical OCF range |
|---|---|
| Index and dividend ETFs | 0.07% – 0.12% |
| Active equity, bond, multi-asset and alternative funds | 0.35% – 0.95% |

The OCF for a specific fund is stated on its factsheet.

## 5. Total cost of ownership

The three layers combine. For a balanced £1,400,000 portfolio holding mostly
active funds:

| Component | Annual cost |
|---|---|
| Advisory fee | £12,050 |
| Platform fee (capped) | £1,200 |
| Weighted fund OCF at approximately 0.60% | £8,400 |
| **Total** | **£21,650** |

That is an all-in cost of roughly **1.55% per year**. Advisers must quote the
all-in figure, not the advisory fee alone, whenever a client asks what they pay.

## 6. How fees are collected

Advisory and platform fees are calculated monthly on the last business day,
using that day's valuation, and deducted from the account's cash balance in
arrears. They appear as transactions of type `fee`.

Where an account holds insufficient cash, the fee is taken from the client's
largest cash balance across their other accounts. Where no account holds
sufficient cash, the firm sells units from the largest holding to raise it, and
notifies the client within five business days.

## 7. VAT

Advisory fees are exempt from VAT where the service consists of intermediation.
Pure advice with no intermediation is subject to VAT at the standard rate. The
firm's standing position is that its ongoing advisory service is exempt.

Transaction charges and the platform fee are exempt.

## 8. Changes to this schedule

The firm gives **30 days' written notice** before any increase in the advisory
fee, platform fee or transaction charges. Reductions take effect immediately and
without notice.
