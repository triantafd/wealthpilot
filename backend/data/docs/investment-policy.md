# Investment Policy Statement

**WealthPilot Advisers Ltd** — fictional firm, synthetic document. Nothing in this
file constitutes advice.

## 1. Purpose

This statement sets the strategic asset allocation for every discretionary and
advisory portfolio managed by the firm. It is the reference point for three
questions: whether a portfolio is positioned correctly for its client, whether a
proposed trade is permitted, and whether a rebalance is required.

Where this statement and a client's recorded restrictions conflict, the
restrictions take precedence. See *Client Investment Restrictions*.

## 2. Governance

The Investment Committee owns this statement and reviews it annually, in January.
Interim amendments require the approval of two committee members, one of whom
must be the Chief Investment Officer. Amendments are versioned; the version in
force is shown above.

Advisers may not deviate from the allocation bands in section 4 without a
documented exception approved by the Investment Committee. Exceptions expire
after 90 days unless renewed.

## 3. Permitted asset classes

Six asset classes are permitted. Anything outside this list requires Investment
Committee approval before purchase.

| Asset class | Description |
|---|---|
| `equity` | Listed equity funds, including single-sector funds |
| `bond` | Corporate bond, gilt and short-duration bond funds |
| `fund` | Multi-asset, balanced and diversified funds |
| `etf` | Index, dividend and sector exchange-traded funds |
| `alternative` | Infrastructure, property and absolute return funds |
| `cash` | Cash and near-cash balances |

Direct holdings in individual company shares, unlisted securities, derivatives,
structured products and crypto-assets are **not permitted** in any mandate.

## 4. Strategic asset allocation

Each client is assigned one of four risk tolerance categories by the suitability
process. The target allocation for that category applies to every account held
under an advisory or discretionary mandate.

### Conservative

| Asset class | Target | Permitted range |
|---|---|---|
| Bond | 55% | 45% – 65% |
| Fund | 25% | 15% – 35% |
| Equity | 15% | 5% – 25% |
| Cash | 5% | 0% – 15% |

### Balanced

| Asset class | Target | Permitted range |
|---|---|---|
| Equity | 40% | 30% – 50% |
| Bond | 35% | 25% – 45% |
| Fund | 20% | 10% – 30% |
| Cash | 5% | 0% – 15% |

### Growth

| Asset class | Target | Permitted range |
|---|---|---|
| Equity | 60% | 50% – 70% |
| Fund | 20% | 10% – 30% |
| Bond | 15% | 5% – 25% |
| Alternative | 5% | 0% – 15% |

### Aggressive

| Asset class | Target | Permitted range |
|---|---|---|
| Equity | 70% | 60% – 80% |
| Alternative | 15% | 5% – 25% |
| Fund | 10% | 0% – 20% |
| Bond | 5% | 0% – 15% |

An asset class not listed for a category has a target of 0% and a permitted
range of 0% – 5%. A conservative portfolio may therefore hold up to 5% in
alternatives, but this is incidental and must not be purchased deliberately.

## 5. Concentration limits

These limits apply to every portfolio regardless of risk category, and are
measured at account level on the valuation date.

| Limit | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Maximum in any single instrument | 20% of account value |
| Maximum in any single fund house | 35% of account value |
| Maximum in any single sector fund | 15% of account value |
| Maximum in a single geographic region other than Global | 60% of account value |
| Minimum number of instruments per account | 4 |

A client-specific concentration limit recorded in their restrictions overrides
the 20% single-instrument limit where it is **stricter**. A client limit may
never be used to permit a larger position than this section allows.

## 6. Rebalancing triggers

A portfolio requires rebalancing when any of the following is true on the
valuation date:

1. Any asset class is outside its permitted range in section 4.
2. Any concentration limit in section 5 is breached.
3. Cash exceeds 15% of account value for more than 30 consecutive days.
4. The client's risk tolerance category has changed since the last rebalance.

Trigger 1 is assessed on the account's total value including cash. A portfolio
within every range but away from target does **not** require rebalancing; drift
within a band is expected and trading it away incurs unnecessary cost.

## 7. Review

Portfolios are reviewed against this statement quarterly, on the first business
day of January, April, July and October. A review that finds a breach must
either produce a rebalancing proposal within 10 business days or record an
approved exception.

The outcome of every review is recorded against the account, including reviews
that find no breach.
