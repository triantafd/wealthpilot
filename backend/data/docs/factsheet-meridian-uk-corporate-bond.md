## Objective

The fund seeks income with a secondary objective of capital preservation, by
investing in sterling-denominated investment-grade corporate bonds issued by UK
companies. It does not hold sub-investment-grade debt.

## Suitability

As a diversified bond fund with no sector classification, this fund is **not
caught by any sector exclusion**. A client who excludes Energy may still hold
it, even though some underlying issuers operate in energy — the exclusion
applies to the fund's sector classification, not to look-through holdings.

The fund is appropriate across all four risk tolerance categories, and is the
largest single component of the conservative target allocation, which holds 55%
in bonds.

Because of its lower volatility, it is commonly used to bring a portfolio back
inside its bond allocation band during a rebalance.
