# Compliance Questions and Answers

**WealthPilot Advisers Ltd** — fictional firm, synthetic document. Answers here
summarise the detailed documents and point to them; where they differ, the
detailed document governs.

## Mandates and authority

**Can I rebalance an execution-only account if it is clearly outside its
allocation bands?**

No. Never, under any circumstances. Write to the client setting out the breach
and record that you did. The decision is theirs. See *Mandates, Authority and
Rebalancing Procedure*, section 2.

**A discretionary client has told me verbally to sell everything. Can I?**

Yes, but record the instruction the same business day. A discretionary mandate
already permits you to sell; the instruction matters because it evidences why
the portfolio left its allocation bands.

**My advisory client gave blanket consent at onboarding to "do whatever is
needed". Is that enough for a rebalance?**

No. Advisory consent must be to a specific set of trades. Generic consent is not
sufficient. See *Mandates*, section 3.

**A proposal was approved three weeks ago but never executed. Can I execute it
now?**

No. Internal approval lapses after five business days and an advisory proposal
expires after ten. Regenerate it against current valuations and seek approval
again.

## Thresholds

**I want to place a £300,000 trade. Who approves it?**

The Head of Investments, countersigned by Compliance. Above £250,000 requires
both. See *Mandates*, section 4.

**Two trades of £20,000 each on the same account on the same day — does that
need approval?**

Yes. Thresholds are cumulative per account per business day, so this is a
£40,000 proposal and needs one senior adviser.

## Suitability

**The client's risk score says aggressive but their capacity for loss is low.
Which wins?**

Capacity for loss, always. You may not recommend a portfolio whose risk exceeds
the client's capacity to absorb a loss, however willing they are. See
*Suitability Assessment Framework*, section 1.

**A client's review is 20 days overdue. Is that a breach?**

Not yet. A review becomes a reportable breach once it is more than 60 days
overdue. It should still be completed promptly.

**Can I buy for a client whose suitability assessment has expired?**

No. You may hold, and you may sell at their instruction, but no purchase and no
rebalance. See *Suitability Assessment Framework*, section 8.

**A client's record shows aggressive tolerance and a preservation objective.
Which do I act on?**

Neither. That combination is not permitted, so the record is wrong. Correct it
before making any recommendation.

**The client wants a 2-year horizon and an aggressive portfolio.**

A horizon under three years caps the category at conservative, regardless of
score. Explain the cap and record the conversation.

## Restrictions

**My client excludes Energy. Can I buy Meridian UK Corporate Bond, which holds
energy company debt?**

Yes. Sector exclusions apply to the fund's sector classification, not to its
underlying holdings. A bond fund has no sector classification. See *Client
Investment Restrictions*, section 2.

**Same client — can I buy Meridian UK Energy Equity Growth?**

No. That fund's sector is Energy. There is no approval route that permits it.

**A client just added a restriction that their existing holding breaches. Do I
sell it?**

Not automatically. Notify them within five business days with the value and the
estimated tax consequence, ask for an instruction, and act on it. If nothing is
received within 30 days, retain and flag for quarterly review.

**The client's limit is 30% in one instrument. Can I take a position to 25%?**

No. The firm's own 20% limit still applies. The stricter of the two always
governs.

## Fees

**A client asks what they pay. What do I quote?**

The all-in figure: advisory fee plus platform fee plus weighted fund OCF. Never
the advisory fee alone. See *Schedule of Fees and Charges*, section 5.

**A client with £1.4m thinks their rate is 0.70%. Are they right?**

No. The fee is tiered, so 0.70% applies only to the portion above £1,000,000.
Their effective rate is 0.861% and their advisory fee is £12,050.

**Does a rebalancing trade attract a transaction charge?**

Not if it was generated by the firm's quarterly review. Client-requested trades
do attract the charge.

**The account has no cash to pay this month's fee. What happens?**

It is taken from the client's largest cash balance in another account. If no
account has enough, the firm sells from the largest holding and notifies the
client within five business days.

## Accounts

**Can I move a client's GIA holdings into their ISA?**

Not as a transfer. The holdings must be sold and repurchased inside the ISA,
which realises gains and incurs charges. Make sure the client understands this
before proceeding.

**A client has two ISAs. Can they contribute £20,000 to each?**

No. The £20,000 allowance is per individual, across all their ISAs.

**Can a 55-year-old take money out of their SIPP?**

No. Age 57 is the earliest, except on ill-health grounds evidenced by two
independent medical opinions.

**A client has moved abroad. Can they keep contributing to their ISA?**

They may keep the ISA but may not contribute to it. They may contribute to a
GIA.

## Records

**Do I need to record a restriction check that passed?**

Yes. A trade with no restriction check recorded is treated as a breach even if
no restriction was breached. The record of the passing check is the evidence
that the control works.

**Do rejected proposals need keeping?**

Yes, for six years, with who rejected them and why. A rejected proposal
evidences that the control worked and is as important as an executed one.
