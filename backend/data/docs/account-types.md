# Account Types and Allowances

**WealthPilot Advisers Ltd** — fictional firm, synthetic document. The
allowances below are invented for a fictional tax regime and must not be taken
as real.

## 1. The five account types

| Code | Name | Tax treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `ISA` | Individual Savings Account | No tax on growth or income; no tax on withdrawal |
| `JISA` | Junior Individual Savings Account | As ISA; locked until the child turns 18 |
| `SIPP` | Self-Invested Personal Pension | Relief on contribution; taxed on withdrawal |
| `GIA` | General Investment Account | Growth and income taxable |
| `TRUST` | Trust Account | Taxed at trust rates; trustees are the client of record |

## 2. Annual allowances

| Account | Annual contribution allowance |
|---|---|
| `ISA` | £20,000 |
| `JISA` | £9,000 |
| `SIPP` | £60,000 or 100% of relevant earnings, whichever is lower |
| `GIA` | Unlimited |
| `TRUST` | Unlimited |

The ISA and JISA allowances are per individual, not per account. A client
holding two ISAs may contribute £20,000 across both, not £20,000 to each.

The SIPP allowance is reduced for clients with income above £260,000, tapering
by £1 for every £2 of income above that figure, to a minimum of £10,000.

Unused ISA allowance cannot be carried forward. Unused SIPP allowance may be
carried forward for up to three tax years, provided the client was a member of a
registered scheme in those years.

## 3. Withdrawal rules

| Account | When funds may be withdrawn |
|---|---|
| `ISA` | Any time, without penalty |
| `JISA` | Not before the child's 18th birthday, except on terminal illness |
| `SIPP` | Not before age 57 |
| `GIA` | Any time |
| `TRUST` | As permitted by the trust deed |

A withdrawal from an ISA does not restore the allowance it used, unless the ISA
is a flexible ISA. The firm's ISA is **not** flexible.

SIPP withdrawals before age 57 are permitted only on grounds of ill health,
evidenced by two independent medical opinions.

## 4. Eligibility

| Account | Who may hold it |
|---|---|
| `ISA` | Residents aged 18 or over |
| `JISA` | Residents under 18; opened by a person with parental responsibility |
| `SIPP` | Residents under 75 |
| `GIA` | Residents aged 18 or over, and non-residents |
| `TRUST` | Trustees of a validly constituted trust |

A client who ceases to be resident may retain an existing ISA but may not
contribute to it. They may continue to contribute to a GIA.

## 5. Transfers

| Transfer | Permitted |
|---|---|
| ISA to ISA | Yes, without using the allowance |
| JISA to ISA | Automatically at age 18 |
| SIPP to SIPP | Yes, subject to receiving scheme acceptance |
| GIA to ISA | No. Must be sold and repurchased, which realises gains. |
| ISA to GIA | Yes, but the tax shelter is lost permanently |

The GIA-to-ISA restriction is the most common source of client
misunderstanding. Moving assets into an ISA always means a sale and a
repurchase, with transaction charges and a possible tax event.

## 6. Account opening

Accounts are opened once the client's identity is verified, the source of funds
is established, and a valid suitability assessment exists. A `GIA` may be opened
without a suitability assessment provided no purchase is made.

Expected timescales:

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Identity verification | 2 business days |
| Source of funds review | 5 business days |
| Account open and ready to fund | 7 business days |
| First investment after funds clear | 3 business days |

## 7. Fees by account type

The advisory fee and platform fee are charged on the client's total value across
all accounts, not per account. Holding four accounts rather than one does not
increase the advisory fee, and the platform fee cap of £1,200 applies per
client.

The minimum annual advisory fee of £1,500 likewise applies per client.

## 8. Closure

A client may close any account at any time. Where the account holds investments,
the client chooses between selling to cash and transferring holdings in specie
to another provider.

| Closure route | Charge | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Sell to cash and transfer | Transaction charges apply | 10 business days |
| Transfer in specie | £15.00 per line, plus £75.00 account fee | 30 business days |

A `SIPP` cannot be closed with a cash withdrawal before age 57; it may only be
transferred to another registered scheme.
